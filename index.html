The road to a 20-minute meeting

Getting to Taylor's already means fighting the LDP. Yet project groups still drive in for short check-ins that could happen online.

At our own gate
~3 hours

One driver's trip from Bukit Puchong to Bandar Sunway on 15 Oct 2026, after a crash on the LDP near the Taylor's service road jammed the area until midday.

Source: BERNAMA, 15 Oct 2026

Across Kuala Lumpur
18 min for 10 km

And 84 hours a year lost to rush hour. That's three and a half days in a car.

Source: TomTom Traffic Index, Kuala Lumpur 2025

Across Malaysia
18% of greenhouse gas

comes from road transport: 59.4 million tonnes CO2e in 2024, and 97% of all transport emissions.

Source: Asian Transport Observatory, 2025

What you get back

The campaign at a glance

Who it's for

Taylor's students with group projects who drive, get a lift or ride e-hailing to campus, and the group leaders who set the meetings.

Why it's different

Skip the jam, not the meeting. We don't ask you to give anything up. You keep the meeting, and get the travel time back. Lower emissions come with it.
